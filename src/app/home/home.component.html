Fitts Law Test

This website runs a pointing-device speed test adapted from Fitts's Law and ISO 9241-9. It was developed by Asit Parida for CMU HCII course 05-640 and updated by Chaoran Chen for 05-440/640.
On the next page, enter the details of the device you are testing. Measuring the screen diagonal accurately is important.
Tap or click the blue circles as quickly and accurately as possible. If you miss, the circle turns red and waits for a correct selection. Do not resize the window or change device orientation during the test.
At the end, you can display your results and download them in JSON or CSV format. The local application also saves one complete record for each finished formal or demo session.
