Fitts Law Test

This application for Fitts Law data collection was adapted for the UCF class on Interaction Techniques in Fall 2026. It runs a pointing-device speed test adapted from Fitts's Law and ISO 9241-9. It was originally developed by Asit Parida, updated by Chaoran Chen, and adapted to the UCF class by Mykola Maslych.

On the next page, enter the details of the device you are testing. Measuring the screen diagonal accurately is important.

Tap or click the blue circles as quickly and accurately as possible. If you miss, the circle turns red and waits for a correct selection. Do not resize the window or change device orientation during the test.

At the end, you can view your results and download the displayed run summaries as a CSV file. Results remain only in this browser tab and are not uploaded or saved by the application.
